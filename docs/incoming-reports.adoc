= Incoming Research Reports
:toc:

This is the receiving surface for results from related repositories. Copy the compact hand-off from each project here, then verify every referenced theorem, test, file, and assumption locally before using it in Robodog design.

== Intake rules

. Preserve the submitting repository, revision, date, and exact status.
. Separate formal proof, executable test, documentation, and speculation.
. Do not upgrade a result when importing it.
. Record counterexamples and retractions beside positive results.
. Link the result to a Robodog issue or experiment.
. A result is not an implementation requirement until its safety and scope review is complete.

== Required report

[source,yaml]
----
repository: hyperpolymath/example
revision: "commit or tag"
status: PROVEN | PARTIALLY PROVEN | NOT PROVEN | NOT APPLICABLE
claim: "one precise claim"
formal_objects:
  - "definitions or types"
proof_or_test_evidence:
  - file: "path"
    theorem_or_test: "name"
    command: "command, if applicable"
assumptions:
  - "checker soundness"
  - "fault/timing/physical model"
limits:
  - "what is not established"
robodog_relevance: direct | indirect | speculative | none
robodog_integration_proposal:
  - "concrete type, invariant, test, or experiment"
robodog_hand_off: "short summary"
verification:
  checked_by: "name or handle"
  checked_on: "YYYY-MM-DD"
  result: pending | accepted | rejected | needs clarification
  robodog_issue: "link or identifier"
----

== Standing distinctions

* BFT agreement is not physical truth.
* Authentication is not correctness.
* A proof term proves only its declared proposition under its assumptions.
* An information residue does not recover information never received.
* A choreography constrains protocol shape; it does not make a runtime honest.
* A resource bound is not a safety proof.
* Inverse metadata is not unconditional reversibility.
* Rejection with no actuator effect is not necessarily total observational nullity.
